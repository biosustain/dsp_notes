Recordings#

We have a shared Youtube channel with the MoNA group, where we upload tutorials: @MoNAandDSP

Instructions#

1. Record a tutorial#

  • record using MacOS Screenshot tool, or any other way (Teams, Zoom, OBS etc.)

2. Upload to OneDrive and edit in Clipchamp#

  • cut and edit the video in Clipchamp

  • you can open videos directly from OneDrive in Clipchamp

3. Re-open exported video in Clipchamp#

  • re-open the edited and exported video in Clipchamp (to ensure the audio and video are in piece and have the same length)

  • detach the audio
    and save it as a separate file

4. Voice Changeover#

  • to ensure consistency in the voice we use, upload the audio to ElevenLabs and generate the voiceover (defaults with Bill)

5. Edit the video in Clipchamp#

  • upload the new audio to Clipchamp and replace the original audio

  • export the video

6. Upload to Youtube#

  • upload the video to the MoNA Youtube channel

  • add the title, description, and time stamps
