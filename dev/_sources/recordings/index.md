# Recordings

We have a shared Youtube channel with the MoNA group, where we upload tutorials:
[@MoNAandDSP](https://www.youtube.com/@MoNAandDSP)

## Instructions

### 1. Record a tutorial

- record using [MacOS Screenshot](https://support.apple.com/en-eg/guide/mac-help/mh26782/mac) tool,
  or any other way (Teams, Zoom, [OBS](https://obsproject.com/) etc.)

### 2. Upload to OneDrive and edit in Clipchamp

- cut and edit the video in [Clipchamp](https://app.clipchamp.com/)
- you can open videos directly from OneDrive in Clipchamp

### 3. Re-open exported video in Clipchamp

- re-open the edited and exported video in Clipchamp (to ensure the audio and video are
  in piece and have the same length)
- [detach the audio](https://clipchamp.com/en/blog/separate-audio-video/) \
  and save it as a separate file

### 4. Voice Changeover

- to ensure consistency in the voice we use, upload the audio to [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io/)
   and generate the **voiceover** (defaults with Bill)

### 5. Edit the video in Clipchamp

- upload the new audio to Clipchamp and replace the original audio
- export the video

### 6. Upload to Youtube

- upload the video to the MoNA Youtube channel
- add the title, description, and [time stamps](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579?hl=en)
